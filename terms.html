What Widget is

An app for designing widgets and showing them on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center. Widgets are drawn by iOS, which decides how often each one is refreshed, so a widget can be a little behind: a clock or countdown may lag by a minute or so, and a calendar may take a while to show a new event. Please don’t rely on a widget where the exact time or an up-to-the-minute calendar matters.
